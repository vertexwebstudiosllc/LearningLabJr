# Roll It Together: sports and sharing

Choose soccer, basketball, catch, tennis, bowling, or hockey, then one of eight illustrated friends. Friends have varied skin tones, hair and clothing; the group includes glasses, a headscarf, a hearing aid and a wheelchair. Every friend can play every sport. The friend grid shuffles on a fresh entry or replay. Children can change their sport before choosing a friend and change their friend before inviting them.

The child invites their chosen friend, hears an accepting response, and plays six back-and-forth exchanges. Sport-specific courts, equipment and ball trajectories make the activities visually distinct. The player is represented by Teddy. The pass control disappears during the friend's turn, and the friend automatically sends the ball back. Both players share equally; there are no scores, losers or timed demands on the child.

After exchanges two, four and six, children choose either of two kind messages. Both are welcome choices about asking, encouraging, sharing, gentle play or thanking. The chosen friend thanks the child before the completion screen. Caregivers are invited to try gentle real-world passes together, including seated play.

All art is scalable SwiftUI drawing with transparent character backgrounds. New dialogue is bundled Amazon Polly Ruth generative narration. No network or microphone is needed to play. Reduce Motion removes flight animation, and spoken prompts, named controls and accessible turn labels support play without relying on color. Automatic turn transitions cancel on navigation and pause while the app is inactive; session, rally and phase guards reject stale callbacks and repeated input.

Validation covers all 48 sport/friend combinations with both message choices, early and duplicate input, invalid selections, stale animation callbacks, completion and fresh-session defaults. Native image attachments cover all friends and courts. Simulator tests complete a six-turn match, use all six sports, change selections, replay, and exit during a pass.
