# My Feeling Weather

The game uses Happy, Sad, Mad, Worried, Calm and Not sure. Mad is the label in the picker, selected feeling, summary and Ruth's spoken prompts. Cross is not used by the game's live content.

After selecting a feeling and its size, the child sees four distinct choices for that emotion plus Something else:

- Happy: share good news, do a happy dance, invite someone to play, draw a happy picture.
- Sad: sit with a grown-up, cuddle a soft toy, ask for a hug, read a cozy story.
- Mad: squeeze a soft toy, take a quiet pause, tell a grown-up, try a gentle stretch.
- Worried: hold a grown-up's hand, ask what happens next, stay near a grown-up, bring a comfort toy.
- Calm: keep playing quietly, listen to a gentle song, look at a picture book, enjoy a quiet moment.
- Not sure: name feelings together, point to a feeling, sit and think together, choose a feeling later.

These are suggestions, not scored answers. Something else accepts another need; children can also change their feeling. All intensity choices, including Not sure, preserve access to the same supports. Choices do not require a feeling to change or affection to be accepted.

Each support has a speaker button for its label. Selecting it shows a local summary and plays a supportive response before the separate finish button, so the completion narration does not immediately interrupt the response. All new lines use bundled Ruth generative Polly recordings. No choices are persisted, uploaded or sent to another person.

Validation covers all six feelings, four sizes, all 24 tailored supports and Something else, invalid/stale inputs, reset, Mad terminology, available symbols and exact Ruth coverage. A simulator playthrough checks each emotion's distinct choices, spoken labels, review-before-finish, replay, Something else, changing a feeling and reentry.
