# Flower Breaths

Eight self-paced activities replace the single three-breath exercise. The sequence begins with the familiar three comfortable flower breaths, then explores a drifting feather, gentle flower touches, tucking in Teddy, slow turtle steps, pond ripples, quiet listening, and kind words. These are play ideas with a grown-up, not a test of whether a child feels calm.

Children choose when to move on. There are no countdowns, breath holds, pressure measurements, microphone/camera monitoring, or scores for becoming calm. Each level offers a watch-with-a-grown-up option, and normal navigation lets families leave at any point. All feelings remain welcome. Breathing instructions invite comfortable breaths and watching, without forcing breath depth or duration.

The feather and turtle use three highlighted stops. Flower buds and lily pads can be tapped in any order, once each. Teddy accepts a blanket drag or an equivalent accessible button. Quiet listening invites noticing sounds or looking at leaves. All three kind-word choices are accepted and narrated. Each activity ends with a next button, so families may pause. Replay and reentry begin with flower breathing.

Artwork uses local SwiftUI vectors and symbols. Flower opening is a short decorative animation that respects Reduce Motion; it does not pace breathing. All 33 new instructions and feedback lines use bundled Ruth generative Polly recordings.

## Design references

- [ZERO TO THREE: co-regulation with a trusted adult](https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/your-calm-is-their-calm-co-regulation-strategies-for-infants-and-toddlers/).
- [ZERO TO THREE: mindfulness activities for families](https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/mindfulness-practices-for-families/).
- [NHS: comfortable breathing without forcing it](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/). This general guidance informs comfort-first wording; the game is not a treatment or a prescribed breathing program.

## Validation

Native tests cover all eight activities through both play and watching, stale and duplicate actions, unordered flower/pond choices, invalid blanket drops, all kind-word choices, state resets, and exact Ruth coverage. The UI playthrough exercises all activities, the real blanket drag, replay through every watching option, and reopening. Screenshots cover the flower, feather, gentle flowers, Teddy, and pond.
