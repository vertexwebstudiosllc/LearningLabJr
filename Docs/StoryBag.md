# The Story Bag

Twelve randomized storybook settings never repeat within the saved app data, including after leaving an unfinished story. The collection ends once all twelve settings have been shown.

Open three bags in order. Each reveals two random choices from four: a character, a surprising discovery, and an ending. Every choice appears in the book illustration. This creates 768 setting-and-choice combinations. Art uses clean cutouts and native vector illustrations.

After the third choice, Ruth automatically reads the opening and the three selected sentences in order, highlighting each passage. Read again, stop, and new-setting controls remain available. Readback follows real audio completion callbacks rather than timers, respects parent voice settings and background/session interruptions, and shares the existing single narration channel. Any new narration interrupts the current story.

Validation covers all combination data, bag and stale-tap guards, persisted exhaustion, narration coverage, all twelve rendered illustrations, and automatic audio progression plus replay/interruption in the simulator.
