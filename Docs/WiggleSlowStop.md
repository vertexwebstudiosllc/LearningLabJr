# Wiggle, Slow, Stop

A start button begins an automatic movement session. Six movement sets (fingers, arms, toes, shoulders, hands and a little dance) shuffle without repetition; a fresh session avoids the previous first movement. Each set cycles through Wiggle, Slow and Stop, with fresh random durations: 8–12 seconds, 6–9 seconds and 4–6 seconds respectively. A full session lasts about two minutes. No tapping is needed between cues, and no camera or movement tracking is used.

A friendly illustrated Teddy models the pace, with large text and symbol cues as well as color. Reduce Motion keeps the illustration still. Seated movements and playing with a grown-up are encouraged. Pause works at any time; resume repeats the current instruction and gives the full cue duration again. Backgrounding, audio interruption, unplugging headphones and the parental session lock pause play. Returning requires an explicit Resume. Stale timers cannot skip cues after pausing, replaying or leaving.

The Start the music and move button explicitly starts activity music, using the existing bundled peekaboo_bounce_loop_112bpm WAV. Wiggle plays at normal tempo; Slow uses 0.65 playback rate; Stop, pause, completion and navigation silence the activity music. A visible Music on/off control works before and during play. This activity-specific choice does not change the app's ambient background-music preference. Ambient music is suspended while the movement game is open and restored to its previous enabled state on exit. Music ducks underneath Ruth's directions, and playback respects app foreground/session lock state and the device's ambient audio behavior.

Twenty new Ruth generative Polly recordings cover the introduction, pause and eighteen movement directions. Every cue recording is shorter than its minimum interval, leaving response time. The existing Ruth completion recording is reused. All music and narration work offline.

Validation covers 200 complete randomized sessions, all timing ranges, stale/duplicate callbacks, pause/resume, replay exclusions, exact Ruth coverage and recording duration. Audio tests verify real AVAudioPlayer playback, slow tempo, silent Stop/mute/pause, narration ducking, ambient restoration and owner isolation. The simulator test follows all eighteen automatic cues, pauses manually and by backgrounding, toggles music, completes, replays and leaves during play. Cue screenshots are exported for visual review.
