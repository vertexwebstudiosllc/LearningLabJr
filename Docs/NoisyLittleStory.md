# A Noisy Little Story

Twelve selectable adventures cover a farm, band, rain, pets, harbor, train, pretend rocket, picnic, soup, snow, bubble party, and bedtime. Each has two narrated pages with two sound pictures. Both pictures must be explored before turning the page; each sound can be repeated freely. The ending returns directly to the story chooser. Children may also leave an unfinished story and choose another.

Completed adventures are removed from the current collection. After the collection is explored, it refreshes without the immediately preceding story. The last completed story is saved locally so reopening does not offer it straight away.

Ruth narrates all page prompts and sound imitations. Brief sound lines use playful delivery. Art uses transparent cutouts from the cleaned asset families and native vector props; no legacy opaque animal images are used.

Validation checks all twelve adventures, page gates, repeated taps, chooser transitions, collection refresh and reopening, every Ruth lookup, an artwork contact sheet, and a full twelve-story UI walkthrough.
