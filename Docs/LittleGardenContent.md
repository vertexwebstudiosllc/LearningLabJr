# Little Garden

Children choose a sunflower, daisy, zinnia, or marigold, then an indoor pot or outdoor garden. Each route has eleven care actions and remains visible at full bloom. Grow another flower resets both choices; leaving and reopening starts a fresh garden.

The indoor route selects a draining pot, adds potting mix, plants and covers a seed, waters gently, and finds a sunny window. The outdoor route finds a sunny spot, loosens soil, plants and covers a seed, waters gently, and adds a flower marker. Both show roots, a sprout, more leaves, a bud, and an open flower. Later indoor care checks dry soil; outdoor care notices rain and clears weeds with a grown-up. Narration makes clear that many days pass and that watering depends on the soil, rather than encouraging constant watering.

The illustrations use SwiftUI shapes, with distinct flower colors and petal arrangements, an indoor window and pot, and an outdoor bed and marker. A labeled cutaway reveals the seed and roots under the soil. There are no timers or precision gestures; children advance at their own pace. Reduce Motion disables growth-transition animation. The shared narrator supplies replayable Ruth directions and respects parent voice settings.
