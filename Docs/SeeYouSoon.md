# See You Soon

Eight selectable pretend stories cover bedtime, school, daycare, leaving friends for dinner, leaving the park, ending a visit, and bathroom goodbyes for poo and pee.

Each story starts by dragging the child to a highlighted destination. Tapping the child and then the destination offers an equivalent accessible interaction. Only the child illustration appears in the drag preview. The eight diverse buddies cycle without repeats within a deck, including across the reshuffle boundary.

Three shuffled goodbye choices include two context-appropriate responses. Each has a hear button. An unrelated response gives gentle guidance and another try. Finishing actions dim a bedtime light, wave, enter home, play and reunite at school/daycare, or flush and pretend to wash and dry hands. Session badges count finished stories; taking a break never awards one. Replay and reentry reset progress.

The real caregiver stays nearby during pretend play. Drop-off stories invite discussion of the actual caregiver and return plan. Hugs are optional and feelings are not graded.

All spoken prompts, choices, retries, and endings use bundled Ruth generative recordings. This update adds 72 audio clips, with no live AWS call required while playing.

Validation covers the story state machine, stale drag tokens and action events, invalid choices, both accepted choices, buddy variety, pauses, narration coverage, and all eight stories through simulator UI automation, including a real drag and tap fallback.
