# Kindness Garden

Each session shuffles twelve distinct stories from a bank of twenty-four. Stories cover shared toys, turns, invitations, helping tidy, listening, caring for animals and plants, asking before borrowing, and respecting hugs and personal space. The first story differs from the previous session's first story. Two illustrated choices appear in shuffled order, with Ruth speaker buttons for their labels.

The pretend garden starts with three flowers. A caring choice adds one flower, up to fifteen. An unhelpful choice fades one, stopping at zero. At zero, the soil and seeds remain and the feedback calls it a resting garden. Flowers visibly grow or fade, and the screen returns to the top so the change is visible even after scrolling to a choice. Reduce Motion removes the growth/fade animation.

Ruth explains what the action does. After an unhelpful choice, the child may try the caring choice or move to another story. Retrying restores growth. The already-tried unhelpful choice is disabled on retry; duplicate input cannot remove extra flowers or farm growth. The garden stays visible on completion, including when empty. Replay or reopening starts with three flowers and fresh stories.

Feedback addresses actions, not whether a child is good or bad. Caring includes respecting no and personal space; hugs and sharing personal comfort items are not required. Caregivers can discuss each pretend consequence together. All narration is bundled Ruth generative audio; no microphone or camera is used.

Validation covers 300 complete randomized sessions: all caring choices, all unhelpful choices down to zero, and every unhelpful choice followed by repair. It checks all 24 stories, shuffled choice positions, invalid/stale/duplicate input, growth bounds, exact Ruth coverage and available symbols. Image attachments cover empty, faded, initial, growing and full gardens. A simulator test plays twelve stories, empties and regrows the garden, checks retry and visibility, then verifies completion, replay and reentry.
