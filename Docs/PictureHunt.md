# Picture Hunt

Sixteen shuffled, self-contained story scenes replace the original three taps on a fixed picnic set. The child sees the illustrated story throughout the round, then finds its three pictured items among six shuffled choices. Answers can be selected in any order. All three must be found before continuing; unrelated choices invite another look, and duplicate taps cannot add progress.

Scenes: Bunny’s picnic, a busy farm, a cozy goodnight, a beach day, a space adventure, a little garden, a rainy walk, a tiny concert, soup for supper, a snowy hello, Cat’s quiet corner, a seaside swim, a birthday surprise, a breezy park, a workshop, and a train trip.

Each scene has three distinct target objects and three absent distractors. Scene art and choices share the same clean cutouts or vector props. The scenery is composed for each story; it is not an answer-card grid. Found objects receive checkmarks in the scene. Each choice has a separate button to hear its name.

A playthrough visits all sixteen scenes once. Replay and reentry reset progress and avoid starting with any of the last three viewed scenes. Only recent scene IDs are stored; no child information is collected. A manual Next story button lets the child look and talk for as long as they like.

Narration uses 62 new bundled Ruth generative recordings plus existing Ruth clips. All stories, item names, retry guidance, and celebration lines are covered. Playback requires no network requests.

Validation checks scene/choice uniqueness, target/decoy separation, available art, Ruth coverage, all sixteen scene renders, randomized playthroughs, premature advancement, duplicate choices, replay, and reentry. The simulator run plays all sixteen scenes, tries an absent item each round, finds the correct items in reverse order, and checks completion and fresh sessions.
